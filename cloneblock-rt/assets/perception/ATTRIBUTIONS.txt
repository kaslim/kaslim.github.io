SCIENTIFIC SCOPE
The two ECAPA counterexamples were selected after reproduction. Scores are ECAPA cosine to independent enrollment prototypes, not the papers' direct reference-to-clone SIM. A score decrease is not a calibrated probability of human identity failure. Both selected protected inputs had audible noise costs; they are not input-preserving protection successes. One exploratory developer listener does not establish a population estimate or method-wide failure.
The two OpenVoice examples are v41 per-recording, 64-step waveform optimization pilots with fixed model weights and native donor-condition matching. They were rated by one non-blind developer listener. They are not results of the later v46 six-coefficient joint search, not streaming real-time protection results, not ABX inaudibility, and not cross-model protection guarantees.
Method plots are our visualizations of v46 p257/reference_1, JOINT_WORST30. The 256-coordinate heatmaps are16x16 display reshapes, not native spatial maps. The two models' spaces are not aligned merely because they have the same dimension. The x10 perturbation view is magnified. The B image compresses time rows and clips color at the99.5th percentile for visibility. smalldata.json contains only320 sampled positions for display and cannot reconstruct the complete WAV or reproduce the constrained search.
